## Scope, provenance and exact-record verification

Read-only [auditor](../../src/dmvmat_ecg/phase2d/annotation_audit.py) re-verifies
publisher/cached metadata hashes via the unchanged folds1–9 loader. Streaming
CSV processing checks the fold before decoding diagnostic dictionaries; original
fold10 diagnostic strings, reports, prediction arrays and waveforms are not
inspected. Only eligible original train/validation metadata enter the analysis.
The SCP dictionary is mapped independently to CD/STTC and **every one of19,230
eligible records' two labels matches its exact cached ECG ID and patient ID**.
No patient-level OR, confidence threshold, diagnosis rewrite or data-driven
record replacement was applied. Source metadata SHA-256 remains
`7600de9c1b27d181d850b3c6038a35d7c3ddb6bb33b702e3a20252a6859d216b`.

The age18–89 eligibility and existing lowest-eligible-ECG-ID policy yield16,415
canonical patients from18,857 age-eligible records;1,784 have multiple eligible
recordings. The625 discordant patients have **1,593 eligible recordings**.
All other records remain excluded rather than migrating across roles. Age is
assigned from the retained record; the10 previously flagged serial age-boundary
crossers still cannot appear in both domains.

## Recommendation and cases needing another policy

**Recommend formal adoption of the existing exact-record canonical policy for
this internal recorded-annotation benchmark**, subject to human acceptance of
its limits. Serial timing, different bytes and varying report metadata make
changing ECG findings and reader/processing variation plausible explanations;
none is proven causal. Canonical selection is solely age eligibility then minimum
ECG ID per patient; no prediction, favorable outcome, prevalence quota, confidence
or selective-transfer result enters that key. Retained labels are the annotations
of that exact ECG, not accumulated lifetime diagnoses.

No automatic exclusion/masking is justified solely by serial disagreement. If
the intended endpoint changes to clinically adjudicated findings, the same-day
pairs, unknown-confidence statements and incomplete ascertainment need expert/
curator review with retained uncertainty masks. Determining which individual
record is wrong cannot be done from these metadata. Missing/invalid patient keys,
ambiguous cross-person duplicates or an exact-record/cache mismatch would block
preparation; none was found here. Confidence-based exclusions, a different
canonical policy or new clinical masks require a **new explicit amendment and
metadata feasibility audit**, not edits to the seed44301 candidate.
