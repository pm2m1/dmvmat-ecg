## Clarifications requiring human acceptance, not experiment expansion

1. **Foundation wording:** historical five-view-versus-flat AUROC has positive
   conditional evidence; AP superiority/noninferiority is unestablished. This is
   sufficient motivation for a mechanism pilot, not a proven best backbone.
2. **Power:** use non-O counts for planning. About757/798 O positives are estimates
   for the unsigned design, not newly audited outcomes. The existing study has
   no demonstrated80% power for+.005 macro AP; plausible paired-AP MDEs exceed
   .03. Describe feasibility/conditional effect-size estimation, and distinguish
   absence of evidence from no meaningful effect. No test-size/seed shopping or
   power-driven outcome access. Any larger study needs its own prospective budget.
3. **Abstention inclusion:** state expressly that all predetermined A episodes,
   including all-SHARE/head-only or E-selected epoch-zero procedures, enter the
   primary mean. A failed technical fit triggers a counted retry/incomplete
   report, not deletion of an unfavorable result.
4. **Random control:** document architecture-specific equality of parameter cost
   and module count, and its one/three eligible-mask limitation. There are at
   most two underlying seeded mask choices with reuse across tiers, not four
   independent random mask replications. Log module count, cost, set size and
   reference-equality. An explicit joint cost/cardinality assertion is reasonable
   future-proofing for the unchanged architecture; it must not change eligible
   masks or consume extra fits in this study. Broader random controls are outside
   the current budget and require a separate design.
5. **Domain realism:** call this an internal same-dataset age-subgroup annotation
   pilot. Do not assert one hospital without provenance. Report source/non-O
   target prevalence, acknowledge prior/co-occurrence shift and unmeasured
   waveform/representation shift. No domain change is proposed.
6. **Storage:** Desktop is not proven cloud-free by the inspected paths/status.
   Confirm actual Desktop & Documents/provider settings and record a suitable
   authoritative-ledger storage decision before training. No workspace move or
   iCloud change is applied or authorized by this draft.
